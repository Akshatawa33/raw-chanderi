Returns

Unworn saris with their tags can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Tell us through the form below with your order number and we will arrange a pickup.

Stitched blouses are made to your size, so they cannot be returned. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of the sari reaching us.
